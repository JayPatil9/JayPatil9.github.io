# Hi, I'm Jay 👋

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Electrical Engineering, but my curiosity has never really respected the boundaries of my syllabus.

By day, I am studying some crazy core EE topics. But by night, I am just as likely to be puzzling over some codeforces problem, or lost in exploring a completely different domain.

As I spend my time jumping between completely independent disciplines, my brain naturally searches for correlations between them.

I realized that the things I was exploring, the lines I was drawing between my engineering classes, my coding sessions, and my math textbooks might actually be useful, or at least interesting, to someone else.

That realization is the sole reason this website exists.

---

This space is my digital notebook, and it serves exactly one purpose: **exploring the hidden bridges between completely independent topics.**

We are often taught to keep our knowledge in neat, separate boxes: mathematics in one, engineering in another, computer science in a third. But the real world doesn't operate in silos, and the most fascinating discoveries rarely happen in isolation. They happen at the borders where two different fields collide.

Here, I try to dismantle those boxes. 

My goal isn't just to teach a concept, but to give you a "eureka" moment by showing you how a tool from one discipline acts as a skeleton key for another.
